# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and collaborate in good faith.
- Focus feedback on ideas and implementation details.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or hateful conduct.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or intimidation.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, or contributions that violate this code.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately via the contact process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
